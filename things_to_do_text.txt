Final Target
The final paper should tell the following story:
Conventional FBG strain–temperature decoupling relies on multiple sensors or purely data-driven regression models. Existing Physics-Informed Neural Networks primarily target PDE-constrained scientific computing. This work bridges instrumentation and scientific machine learning by embedding the analytical FBG measurement equation directly into a neural network loss function, thereby formulating strain–temperature decoupling as an inverse measurement problem. Through comprehensive benchmarking against classical and modern machine learning methods, extensive robustness evaluation, and rigorous statistical validation, the proposed framework demonstrates accurate, physically consistent, and practical single-sensor sensing suitable for next-generation intelligent instrumentation.
